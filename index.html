DWG-05 — PROFILE

About

I'm a freelance web designer working at the intersection of design and front-end code — which means I can take a project from a blank page to a live, working site without losing anything in the handoff.

Most of my clients are small businesses and independent studios who need a site that looks like it was made on purpose, not assembled from a template. I care about the details that don't show up in a screenshot: load time, how the type breathes at every screen size, whether the checkout flow actually converts.

Based remotely, working with clients everywhere. Currently booking projects starting late 2026.
